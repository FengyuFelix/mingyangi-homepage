I'm a Ph.D. student at Dalian University of Technology (DUT), China, advised by **Prof. Yanqing Guo**. I'm also a visiting student at Monash University, working with the **Theta Team**, an AI for Biology team co-supervised by **Prof. Shirui Pan** and **A/Prof. Vivek Naranbhai**. I maintain a close and ongoing collaboration with Assistant Professor **Xin Zheng** from RMIT University.

My research focuses on: **(1) AI for Biology; (2) Protein Design.** I work on machine-learning methods for computational protein design and related problems at the interface of AI and biomedicine, with papers accepted by ICML, KDD, and PAKDD.
